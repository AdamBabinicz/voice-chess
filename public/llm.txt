# ChessTactics Audio Coach
> Voice-first, accessible chess training web application with blindfold practice, speech recognition, and instant tactical audio feedback.

## Overview
ChessTactics Audio Coach enables visually impaired players, blindfold practitioners, and hands-free chess students to play chess, solve tactical puzzles, and receive pedagogical coaching using natural voice commands in English and Polish.

## Core Capabilities
- Natural Voice Input: Voice command recognition for chess moves via Web Speech API (English and Polish algebraic notation).
- Tactical Audio Coach: Real-time heuristic evaluation explaining center control, piece coordination, pins, forks, and threats.
- Master AI Analysis: Deep Grandmaster-level positional assessment powered by Google Gemini API.
- Blindfold Visualization Mode: Complete board shroud with 3-second emergency peek and spoken board position reader.
- Tactical Puzzles: Curated tactical training positions (Knight Forks, Back Rank Mate, Absolute Pins, Skewers, Discovered Attacks).
- Accessibility: 100% WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, screen-reader friendly, and keyboard operable.
