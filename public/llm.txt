# ChessTactics Audio Coach

Voice-first chess training for complete games, tactical puzzles, blindfold practice, and accessible audio coaching.

## Core features
- Interactive chess board with move selection and algebraic move log
- Web Speech API-ready audio coach experience
- Blindfold mode, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader-friendly labels
- English and Polish interface
- Light and dark display themes
